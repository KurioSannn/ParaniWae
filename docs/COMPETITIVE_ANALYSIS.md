# Competitive Landscape - SILATARA

**Scope:** public product listings and academic prior art identified during desk research through 19 November 2026. This is not an exhaustive global patent/market scan.

## 1. Competitive framing

SILATARA is not competing only with pencak silat apps. Its alternatives include:
- YouTube/social-media tutorials;
- school-based in-person teaching;
- static learning apps and video libraries;
- AR/multimedia learning systems;
- pose-estimation research prototypes;
- competition/scoring systems for artistic single routines;
- generic martial arts or fitness apps.

## 4. Defensible differentiation

The opportunity is not a novel pose detector by itself. It is a product/system integration with evidence:

1. **Partner-curated knowledge provenance:** every tradition/movement is sourced, attributed, versioned, and access-controlled.
2. **Style-aware standards:** the assessment references only a particular partner-approved movement/rubric, not a universal standard for all styles.
3. **Explainable instructional feedback:** per-phase corrections originate in instructor-approved rules, not arbitrary LLM output.
4. **Learning loop:** video lesson -> camera practice -> feedback -> repeat -> progress -> in-person school pathway.
5. **Cultural and social outcome evaluation:** measure learning, cultural awareness, confidence, and actual school participation, not only computer-vision accuracy.
6. **Partner value:** instructors and schools control their material and can receive attribution, leads, and fair revenue opportunities.

This is a proposed integrated value proposition. Its novelty must be reassessed against a fresh literature/market search before a journal submission or patent claim.

## 6. Competitive risks and responses

- **Risk:** a competitor already offers the feature.  
  **Response:** avoid “first” claims; compare user outcome, partner governance, curriculum, assessment reliability, and measured impact.
- **Risk:** a study reports higher pose-classification accuracy than SILATARA.  
  **Response:** do not compare headline percentages across different datasets; run an agreed evaluation on a partner-validated dataset.
- **Risk:** one school's rubric is mistakenly represented as national standard.  
  **Response:** show partner/style scope and rubric version in every learning and assessment screen.
- **Risk:** directory becomes generic and unverified.  
  **Response:** only list partners who have agreed to be listed and provide an official contact/review date.
