# Research Brief - Pencak Silat, BUDAYA GO!, and SILATARA

**Research cut-off:** 19 November 2026  
**Purpose:** provide a traceable evidence base for product decisions, not a claim of exhaustive national inventory or a completed systematic review.

## 1. What the research says about pencak silat as cultural heritage

UNESCO's element page records **Traditions of Pencak Silat** as inscribed in 2019 on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. The UNESCO description says the tradition includes sporting, mental-spiritual, self-defence, and artistic aspects. It also describes regional differences in movement, style, accompaniment/music, costume, instruments, and traditional weapons; and notes that related knowledge is taught in non-formal schools alongside oral traditions, greetings, philosophical phrases, rhymed poems, advice, songs, and instrument techniques.

**Product implications:**
- Archive more than a pose/video: preserve context, terminology, music, teaching sequence, instructor source, and permitted access.
- Do not build one universal scoring model and call it the standard for all silat.
- A first partner's style should be represented accurately, with explicit boundaries around what is not covered.
- Human cultural authority is central; computer vision is a learning aid.

Source: UNESCO, `https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditions-of-pencak-silat-01391`.

## 2. Cultural and social problem hypotheses

The founder's stated observations and earlier desk research suggest three hypotheses:

1. Some beginners have an interest in learning pencak silat but face an information/access/confidence barrier before joining a school.
2. Negative perception caused by the conduct of some individuals or groups may shape the social image of pencak silat for some young people.
3. A digital learning entry point could increase cultural awareness and make it easier for interested beginners to progress to a real instructor/community.

These are not national-level facts. Validate with the actual questionnaire, interview transcripts, and partner interviews. Avoid language that labels all perguruan or practitioners as violent, or claims that all Gen Z/Gen Alpha have lost interest.

### Research questions

- Which barriers actually stop interested beginners from trying pencak silat?
- Which barriers are strongest: fear of judgment, access/location, schedule, cost, lack of information, uncertainty about school quality, or something else?
- Do respondents distinguish the conduct of certain individuals/groups from the cultural practice itself?
- What would make respondents comfortable starting at home, and what would make them ready to attend in-person practice?
- Which content should a partner instructor consider safe and appropriate for independent beginner learning?

## 5. Digitalization maturity model

Use this model when auditing existing culture documentation:

1. **Visibility:** basic web pages, photos, static descriptions.
2. **Documentation:** audio, video, written records, digitized manuscripts.
3. **Structured knowledge:** terms, movement phases, sources, variations, materials, context, and metadata.
4. **Interactive transmission:** guided lessons, practice, quizzes, movement feedback.
5. **Community ecosystem:** masters, learners, schools, curators, and events connected.
6. **Sustainable cultural economy:** permissioned products/services, attribution, fair value sharing, and measurable benefit to practitioners.

Do not claim an object is “not digitized” merely because a quick search found few results. Audit existing government platforms, university repositories, partner archives, social channels, and community records first.

## 6. Evidence classification

Use these labels in product research and proposal writing:

- `SOURCE-FACT`: direct statement/data in official Juknis, official BUDAYA GO page, UNESCO, or a source paper.
- `RESEARCH-FINDING`: result reported in an academic study; always include study scope and limitations.
- `USER-REPORTED`: founder/user states a survey/interview/partner contact exists, but raw files have not been inspected.
- `PRODUCT-HYPOTHESIS`: a problem/benefit SILATARA expects to solve; validate it.
- `PRODUCT-DECISION`: selected approach, scope, or design rule.
- `TARGET`: intended metric, not an achieved result.

## 7. What remains unverified

- raw questionnaire response count, sampling method, question wording, distributions, and analysis;
- interview participants, roles, transcripts, quotes, and consent;
- identity, legitimacy, and written commitment of the first partner school/instructor;
- the specific style/lineage, approved curriculum, and safe movements for the MVP;
- rights to training videos, photographs, music, movement documentation, and cultural materials;
- actual AI benchmark results on SILATARA data;
- actual learning outcomes and community conversion;
- brand/trademark/domain availability for SILATARA.

Do not write around these gaps. Collect evidence and update this document.
