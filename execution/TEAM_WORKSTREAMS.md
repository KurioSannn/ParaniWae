# Team Workstreams & Ownership Template

The competition Juknis describes a three-person team: product/prototype capability, economics/business capability, and cultural-substance capability. Map actual team members to these responsibilities; do not invent names or credentials.

| Workstream | Primary owner | Supporting role | Key deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product strategy and PRD | Product lead | All | PRD, roadmap, scope, acceptance criteria |
| UI/UX design | UI designer | Product lead, frontend | Figma, tokens, responsive screens, interaction handoff |
| Frontend implementation | Frontend engineer | UI designer | Next.js app, camera UX, learning, progress |
| Computer vision | CV engineer | Partner instructor, frontend | Landmarks, quality gate, features, assessment tests |
| Backend and database | Backend engineer | Frontend | APIs, auth, persistence, partner/content workflows |
| Cultural research/partner | Cultural lead | Instructor, product lead | Partner evidence, source/permission register, movement validation |
| Business model | Business lead | Partner lead | BMC, pricing experiments, revenue-share framework |
| Research/QA | QA/research lead | CV engineer, partner | Test protocol, expert comparison, usability/learning pilot |
| Competition proposal | Product + business + cultural leads | All | Proposal, deck, showcase video, evidence appendices |

## Collaboration checkpoints

1. **Scope freeze:** product lead and cultural partner approve the first movement list.
2. **Design sign-off:** UI designer + product lead approve flow, copy, and states before major frontend implementation.
3. **Movement rubric sign-off:** instructor approves all criteria/corrections before AI scoring is enabled.
4. **Technical review:** CV/backend/frontend verify pipeline and API contracts.
5. **Pilot review:** QA/research and partner compare AI output and learner outcomes.
6. **Release approval:** product, cultural partner, privacy/safety, and QA sign off.

## Suggested separation of parallel work

- Designer can work in Figma while backend schema/API contracts are drafted.
- CV engineer can build an isolated landmark/assessment prototype while UI shell is built.
- Cultural lead can validate content/rubrics while the software foundation is prepared.
- Do not let multiple developers edit the same assessment files in parallel without agreed ownership.
