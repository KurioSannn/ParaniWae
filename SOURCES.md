## G. Source-use rules

- A paper's reported performance belongs to that paper's dataset and protocol. Do not compare headline accuracy percentages across unrelated datasets.
- Official BUDAYA GO! competition scoring and eligibility should be cited to the official Juknis/website, not blog summaries.
- The 2025 project list is based on the supplied workbook; 14 Juara Bersama works do not have individual ranks in that workbook.
- TRIA technical findings apply only to the supplied gitingest snapshot.
- Do not include personal ID documents or KTP data in the public repository or this product documentation pack.
